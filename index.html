Our story

Over ten years behind the chair

Jason Kenny spent years learning the trade in other people's shops before opening his own in Oldham town centre in 2015. A second shop in Royton followed.

After three years of running both, Jason closed the town-centre shop when its lease ended and put everything into Royton. The Rochdale Road shop was refurbished and made bigger, and it now opens seven days a week.

Long hair, short hair, beards and traditional cut-throat shaving are all part of the job here. Come in for a quick tidy-up or the full treatment.
